Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you choose to provide, including your account details such as name, email address, and password (stored in hashed form), and the expense records you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Collect

We collect information directly from you when you register, sign in, or add and edit expenses. We may also collect limited technical information automatically, such as session data needed to keep you signed in. We do not collect information from your device beyond what is needed to operate the service.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to create and maintain your account, display your expenses and summaries, let you filter and review your spending, and keep the service secure and working properly. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your expense records for advertising.

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored on the servers that run Spendly. We apply reasonable safeguards, such as password hashing and restricted access, to protect it. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute protection. We keep your data for as long as your account is active, and you may ask us to delete your account and associated records.

5. Third Party Services

Spendly may load fonts and other resources from third-party providers, such as Google Fonts, when you visit a page. These providers may receive basic request information, such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies. We do not share your expense records with third parties except where required by law.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to exercise your rights over your data, including access, correction, or deletion, please contact the Spendly team through the contact details published on our website.
